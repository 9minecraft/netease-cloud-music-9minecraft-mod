# NetEase Cloud Music — Broadcast Custom Songs Across Your Base

NetEase Cloud Music 9minecraft Mod turns your world into a personal radio station. It brings blank discs and custom machines that let you burn and play audio tracks in your base. You can write online audio links directly onto Music CDs or broadcast live web radio streams across your town. Nearby players hear the music clearly, watch note particles fly, and read lyrics on their screens.

Music CD: Works as a blank disc that holds audio data, lyrics, and song names for players to trade or store in chests.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **IMG**, **TartaricAcid**, **郝南村**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
